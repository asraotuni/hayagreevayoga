Chandrika Saripalli

MSc Yoga Therapy

Welcome. I’m Chandrika, a yoga therapist who has worked with people across more than 10 countries, supporting them through a wide range of health and wellbeing challenges.

My approach brings together gentle movement, breathing, and relaxation, with space for your individual needs. Wherever you are starting from, the intention is simple: to help you build a more mindful relationship with your body and your wellbeing.
